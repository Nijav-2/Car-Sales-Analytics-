# Car Sales Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

This project showcases an **interactive Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze car sales performance and revenue trends. The report provides key insights into sales metrics, brand performance, and monthly trends using dynamic visuals and filters.

## Dashboard Overview

The report contains a single analytics page presenting important sales KPIs and visual insights.

### Key KPIs

* YTD Cars Sold
* Total Revenue Earned
* Top Sold Car Brand (interactive selection shows the corresponding car model)

### Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard using:

* Month
* Quarter
* Year

### Visualizations

* **Donut Chart:** YTD Total Sales by Car Colour
* **Pie Chart:** YTD Total Sales by Body Style
* **Dealer Sales Table:** Dealer Name, Body Style, YTD Total Sales, PYTD Total Sales, MTD Total Sales, and YoY Growth (%)
* **Monthly Sales Table:** Total cars sold across each month of the selected year
* **Line Chart:** YTD Average Price vs PYTD Average Sales by Month

## Purpose

The dashboard helps analyze car sales performance, track revenue growth, and identify sales patterns across dealers, car styles, and time periods.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX Measures
* Interactive Filters & Visualizations
